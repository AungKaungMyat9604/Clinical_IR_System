The Unstructured EHR Data Challenge

Prior Art & Biomedical NLP Evolution

Probabilistic Baselines

Robertson & Zaragoza (2009)

“The probabilistic relevance framework: BM25 and beyond,” Foundations and Trends in Information Retrieval, vol. 3, no. 4, pp. 333–380, 2009.

Established the BM25 framework, solving length-bias issues in raw term-frequency systems.

Domain Transformers

Lee et al. (2020); Gu et al. (2021)

“BioBERT: a pre-trained biomedical language representation model for biomedical text mining,” Bioinformatics, vol. 36, no. 4, pp. 1234–1240, 2020.

“Domain-specific language model pre-training for biomedical natural language processing,” ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare, vol. 3, no. 1, pp. 1–23, Oct. 2021.

PubMed-domain pre-training (BioBERT, PubMedBERT) outperforms general-domain BERT on clinical text.

The EHR Length Barrier

Gao, Dai, & Callan (2021)

“COCO-DR: Combating the text length challenge in dense text retrieval for clinical domains,” Journal of Biomedical Informatics, vol. 122, p. 103901, Oct. 2021.

Dense models collapse when processing lengthy, un-chunked clinical charts without length-aware retrieval.

6. Ethical & Legal Implications
Zarni Hlawn

Ethical Considerations in Clinical AI

Algorithmic Fairness

MIMIC-IV data is heavily skewed towards a single demographic region (Boston, MA ICU patients). Models learning localized clinical slang may face bias and underperform if deployed globally.

Patient Safety Risk

False positives in clinical cohort retrieval could lead to patients being incorrectly flagged for pharmaceutical trials or misdiagnosed in automated charting, demanding human-in-the-loop validation.

6. Ethical & Legal Implications
Zarni Hlawn

Legal Compliance & Data Governance

  • MIMIC-IV DUA: Execution required strict legal credentialing through PhysioNet and a binding Data Use Agreement.
  • Re-identification Ban: Absolute legal prohibition against attempting to re-identify the de-identified patients in the vector space.
  • HIPAA / GDPR: Processing automated health data requires high-security computing environments to prevent unauthorized vector-reconstruction attacks.
Strict Compliance Maintained Throughout Development

Zarni Hlawn

Conclusion & Architectural Horizons

Our hybrid pipeline effectively balances keyword constraints with specialized clinical embeddings to solve vocabulary mismatch without creating semantic noise.

Future Horizons

Replace unweighted rank fusion layouts with learned linear rank scaling matrices or deploy dense cross-encoders purely as secondary re-ranking steps to mitigate latency.
